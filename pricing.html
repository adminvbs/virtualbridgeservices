How Our Pricing Works

We combine competitive hourly rates with managed support so you get both affordable talent and reliable delivery.

Step 1

Role Based Hourly Rate

Your monthly cost starts with the hourly range of the role you need. Rates vary based on the type of support and specialization required.

Step 2

Fees Structure

Your pricing includes a $300 USD one-time onboarding fee for sourcing, screening, and onboarding, plus a $300 USD monthly management fee covering account management, HR support, and ongoing service oversight.

Step 3

Advance Billing

We bill one month in advance. Invoices are issued 15 days before the service month and are payable within 7 days.
